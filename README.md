# ANT Bogura - App Information

**ANT Bogura** is the official mobile application designed to provide a seamless internet management experience for our customers. This document provides comprehensive information about the app's features, technical details, and installation procedures.

## 📱 About the App

The ANT Bogura app serves as a digital gateway for our subscribers, bridging the gap between users and their internet service. Whether you need to pay a bill, check your speed, or report an issue, everything is accessible in just a few taps.

## ✨ Core Functionalities

### 1. Real-time Monitoring & Usage
- **Live Speed Graphs:** Visualize your current download and upload speeds in real-time.
- **Usage Statistics:** Keep track of your daily and monthly data consumption.
- **Session History:** Review your connection logs to monitor active periods.

### 2. Billing & Payments
- **Integrated Payments:** Securely pay monthly bills via **bKash**, **Nagad**, and more.
- **Payment History:** Access digital receipts and records of all previous transactions.
- **Billing Alerts:** Receive reminders before your bill due date to ensure uninterrupted service.

### 3. Account & Package Management
- **Plan Details:** View your current bandwidth speed, monthly cost, and expiration date.
- **Package Upgrades:** Request a change to a higher-speed plan directly from the app.
- **Profile Security:** Update your account information and change your Wi-Fi/Portal password easily.

### 4. Customer Support
- **Support Tickets:** Open a technical support ticket and track its progress in real-time.
- **Direct Communication:** Access quick links to our WhatsApp and Phone support lines.
